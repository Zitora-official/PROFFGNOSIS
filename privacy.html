1. Information We Collect

When you sign in and complete our one-time student registration form, we collect only the information needed to establish and personalize your MBBS learning profile:

  • Google Account Identifiers: Your Google account identity, unique Google user ID, and Google email address.
  • Student Profile Details: Your full name (as provided during registration), medical college name, MBBS admission year, current MBBS professional year, age, and gender.
  • AI Assistant Interactions: Questions, prompts, and messages you voluntarily submit when interacting with the SHIVOID AI study assistant.
No Phone Number Collection: PROFFGNOSIS does not ask for, collect, or store students' mobile or telephone numbers.

2. How Google Sign-In Works

PROFFGNOSIS uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) facilitated through Supabase Auth so you do not have to create or remember a separate password for our platform.

When you choose to sign in with Google, Google authenticates your identity and shares basic profile authorization details (such as your email address and Google ID) with our authentication system. PROFFGNOSIS never receives or has access to your Google account password.

3. How We Use Student Information

We use the information collected from students strictly to operate and improve the educational experience on PROFFGNOSIS:

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain your individual student account.
  • To tailor study resources and academic context to your MBBS professional year and academic background.
  • To operate educational features, including responding to study queries through SHIVOID AI.
  • To maintain platform integrity, prevent misuse, and respond to student support or account inquiries.

We do not sell student personal information to third parties or use student registration data for third-party advertising.

4. Authentication & Database Services (Supabase)

PROFFGNOSIS relies on Supabase as its backend infrastructure provider for authentication management (Supabase Auth) and database storage. Your Google authentication records and the registration details you submit (name, medical college, admission year, professional year, age, and gender) are stored in our Supabase-hosted database to support application functionality.

5. Platform Hosting (Vercel)

The PROFFGNOSIS web application is hosted on Vercel. When you access our website at https://proffgnosis.vercel.app/, Vercel automatically processes standard technical connection data—such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and request timestamps—to deliver web pages securely, maintain availability, and protect against network abuse.

6. SHIVOID AI & Planned Grok/xAI Integration

PROFFGNOSIS includes an educational AI assistant named SHIVOID, which is planned to utilize Grok by xAI to generate study responses.

When this integration is active and you submit a question or message to SHIVOID, the text of your prompt may be transmitted to and processed by xAI's API infrastructure in order to generate an educational response. Because third-party AI processing involves external servers, please avoid entering sensitive personal details, patient-identifiable clinical records, or confidential information into SHIVOID prompts.

7. Storage & Security Practices

We rely on established cloud infrastructure providers (Supabase and Vercel) and restrict administrative database access to authorized personnel at Zitora Labs. Standard HTTPS transport is used when transmitting data between your browser and our hosted services.

While we take reasonable organizational and technical steps to protect student records, no online platform or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Sharing with Service Providers

We share data only to the extent required to run PROFFGNOSIS:

  • Infrastructure & Auth Providers: Google (for OAuth sign-in), Supabase (for authentication and database hosting), and Vercel (for web hosting).
  • AI Processing Provider: xAI (Grok), when processing messages submitted to SHIVOID AI.
  • Legal Compliance: If required to comply with applicable Indian law, court orders, or valid governmental requests.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your registration profile and account records for as long as your student account remains active on PROFFGNOSIS or as needed to provide the service.

How Account Deletion Works: Currently, PROFFGNOSIS does not offer an automated self-service delete button inside the application. Account deletion is handled manually by a platform administrator. If you wish to delete your account and associated registration data, please email us at zitora.official@gmail.com from your registered Google email address. Once verified, an administrator will process your deletion request within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Your Privacy Rights & Requests

As a student user, you may contact us to:

  • Request access to the registration information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate registration details (such as your college name or current MBBS professional year).
  • Request manual deletion of your account and profile records.
  • Revoke PROFFGNOSIS's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions settings.

11. Cookies, Sessions & Essential Storage

PROFFGNOSIS uses essential browser storage (such as local storage or session tokens managed by Supabase Auth) strictly to keep you signed in across page loads and maintain session security. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral ad cookies.

12. Children's Privacy

PROFFGNOSIS is designed for undergraduate medical (MBBS) students enrolled in higher education. It is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent without parental authorization). If we become aware that personal data from an underage child has been collected without appropriate consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

13. Updates to This Policy

As PROFFGNOSIS evolves—including when planned features such as SHIVOID AI integrations are updated—we may revise this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
